Trio Mosaic – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Trio Mosaic app ("the app") handles. The app is made by Hatice Erciyes ("we"). If you have questions, write to haticeerciyes_7@hotmail.com.

What the app itself keeps

The app has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email, contacts or location. Your game progress and settings (solved puzzles, stars and times, streaks, coins, hints, chosen colours and similar game data) are stored only on your device. We do not receive or store this data on any server. Coins are in-game only and cannot be bought, sold or cashed out.

Uninstalling the app deletes this data. Your device's own backups (iCloud or Google backup) may include it, depending on your device settings.

Reminders

If you turn on the optional daily reminder, the app schedules local notifications on your device. They are created by the app itself, are not sent from any server and contain no personal data. You can turn them off at any time in the app under Settings, or in your device's notification settings.

Play statistics

The app counts anonymous play statistics on your device (for example how long a level took or how many hints were used). They never leave your device unless you choose Settings → Play statistics → Copy and send the copied text to someone yourself.

Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob: optional reward videos you choose to watch, short ads between some levels, and a banner at the bottom of the screen. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's ad software in the app may collect and process information such as:

  • your IP address (which can indicate your approximate location);
  • device and advertising identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID; on iOS, the app does not ask for tracking permission, so Apple's advertising identifier is not used);
  • interactions with ads and the app (for example, ads shown and tapped, app launches);
  • diagnostic information (for example, performance data).

This information is handled by Google, not by us. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Consent. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and in some US states), the app asks for your choices before personalised ads are shown. You can change them at any time in the app under Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • Advertising ID. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Settings → Privacy → Ads. On iOS you can limit ad tracking in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Ad-free. Reward videos are always optional. Not watching them never blocks the game.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. Ads rated for mature (18+) audiences are not shown in the app. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app and personal information was collected through ads, contact us and we will help with a deletion request to Google.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR or US state privacy laws), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of personal information, or to opt out of its "sale" or "sharing" for advertising. Because we do not hold your data, ad-related requests are handled through Google's controls above; you can also contact us and we will help.

Security

Google's ad software sends data over encrypted connections. Game data stays on your device and is protected by your device's own security.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Important changes will also be noted in the app's store listing.
